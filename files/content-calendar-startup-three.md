## Production Notes

- **Voice reminder:** Lead with the business problem. Use the measurement-action loop as the structural spine. End every post with a concrete decision or diagnostic question.
- **Constraint:** No fabricated statistics. All data claims must be sourced at write time.
- **Word count target:** 1,000–2,000 words per post.
- **Never frame employees as costs** — development is a growth lever, always.
- **Distinguish assessment from surveillance** explicitly wherever monitoring or tracking language is adjacent.
